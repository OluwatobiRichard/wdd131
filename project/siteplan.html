Site Information

Site Name

Tech_Tee Fitness Hub

Description

Tech_Tee Fitness Hub is a local gym website designed to help people improve their health and fitness through professional training programs, modern equipment, and expert coaching. The name combines your personal brand, Tech_Tee, with fitness services, creating a memorable and unique identity.

Optional Domain Name

techteefitnesshub.com

Site Purpose

Tech_Tee Fitness Hub provides information about gym memberships, fitness programs, personal training services, workout schedules, and health tips. Visitors can learn about the gym, view available services, check operating hours, and contact the gym for membership registration or inquiries.

Scenarios

  1. What membership plans does Tech_Tee Fitness Hub offer, and how much do they cost?
  2. What are the gym's operating hours, and how can I contact a fitness trainer?
  3. Does the gym provide personal training and weight-loss programs?
  4. What equipment and facilities are available at the gym?

Color Schema

Since fitness websites usually communicate strength, energy, and motivation, these colors work well:
Website Color
Color Hex Code Usage
Dark Navy #0A192F Header, Footer, Navigation
Orange #F97316 Buttons, Call-to-Action Areas, Highlights
White #FFFFFF Background
Light Gray #F3F4F6 Section Backgrounds

Color Usage

  • Dark Navy (#0A192F): Header, footer, and navigation menu.
  • Orange (#F97316): Buttons, links, hover effects, and important information.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Main page background.
  • Light Gray (#F3F4F6): Alternate content sections.

Typography

Font 1: Poppins

Poppins will be used for:

  • Headings
  • Navigation
  • Buttons

Font 2: Roboto

Roboto will be used for:

  • Paragraphs
  • General content
  • Forms
