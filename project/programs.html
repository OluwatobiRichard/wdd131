How It Works

From Assessment to Results

Every member starts with the same three steps, no matter which program they choose. It keeps training honest and progress measurable.

1. Assess

A baseline fitness and mobility check so your coach knows exactly where you're starting from.

2. Train

Structured sessions matched to your chosen program, intensity, and weekly availability.

3. Track

Monthly check-ins and biometric tracking so adjustments happen before plateaus do.
